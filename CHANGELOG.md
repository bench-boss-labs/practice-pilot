# Practice Pilot — changelog

## 1.1.2 — 2026-11-05 (Easier passing and a bigger preview)

- **New:** Pass and Shoot: tap the player with the puck, tap Pass and then the teammate, or Shoot and then the spot. A pass goes to where the teammate ends up and waits until they arrive.
- **New:** Resize the drill preview in the library by dragging the handle beside it, or tap Expand to hide the cards. While a preview is open, the cards shrink to a list.
- **New:** The Step tab lists what's moving in the step. Everything in a step moves at the same time.
- **Improved:** Easier to pick a puck on a player's stick: tap the player again to switch to the puck, or tap Select the puck.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-11-05 (Drill setup)

- **New:** Every drill has a Setup list telling other coaches how to prep the ice, such as "Pucks on both blue lines" or "Pylons on the dots". It shows in the preview above the coaching points, and search looks in it too.
- **Improved:** The three sample drills now come with setup lines.
- **Fixed:** Adding a new coaching point no longer clears the one you just typed.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-04 (Drill library and editor)

- **New:** Build drills that move: place players, pucks and equipment, add steps, and drag players to make them skate, carry the puck, pass and shoot.
- **New:** Curved paths, stops and pivots, and passes that wait until skaters arrive.
- **New:** Callouts that follow a player, and a glow on the player to watch.
- **New:** Draw on a drill with pen, arrows, boxes and text.
- **New:** Tags, age groups, level, coaching points, video links and goalie notes for every drill.
- **New:** A library with search, filters and a playing preview, plus three sample drills to start.
- **New:** Templates, Duplicate, Undo and Redo, and Recently deleted, which keeps deleted drills for 30 days.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-04 (Install button fix)

- **Fixed:** The Install button no longer shows once Practice Pilot is installed on your device.
- **Improved:** Updated to Bench Boss Labs kit 1.0.1.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04 (Practice Pilot is set up)

- **New:** Practice Pilot installs on your tablet or phone and opens with no internet, even at a rink with no signal.
- **New:** Help, with what's new in each update, and a full guide.
- **New:** Settings › This device: Install (a button, or the steps for your device), your team name, Dark or Light theme, and Back up now / Restore to keep your work safe.
- **New:** When there's an update, an amber banner says what's in it, with Update now and Later.
