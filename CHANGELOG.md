# Practice Pilot — changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-04 (Install button fix)

- **Fixed:** The Install button no longer shows once Practice Pilot is installed on your device.
- **Improved:** Updated to Bench Boss Labs kit 1.0.1.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04 (Practice Pilot is set up)

- **New:** Practice Pilot installs on your tablet or phone and opens with no internet, even at a rink with no signal.
- **New:** Help, with what's new in each update, and a full guide.
- **New:** Settings › This device: Install (a button, or the steps for your device), your team name, Dark or Light theme, and Back up now / Restore to keep your work safe.
- **New:** When there's an update, an amber banner says what's in it, with Update now and Later.
